A closer look

How I build

  • One job, done well

    An app should work without setup pain or a manual.

  • Your data stays yours wherever possible

    On your device first, your iCloud second, and a server only when the app truly needs one. No accounts when your Apple ID will do.

  • Run it like production

    Small releases, real testing, and checking what happens after launch. That habit comes from years in operations.

The test I use

Would my wife use it without me having to explain anything first?

I started on a neurosurgery research team at the University of Pittsburgh and went on to direct 24x7 operations. Then came seven years at Apple Retail, a warehouse and a pro desk. I’ve seen how big systems stay up, and how technology looks to the person standing in front of it.
