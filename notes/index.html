How the apps started

Daily Do Brief

My mornings meant checking the calendar, the mail, the packages and everything due, app by app. So I put it all on one large-print page I can read on my phone or print.

Xgest

It started in my driveway, squinting at a warning light I couldn’t name, with a 532-page PDF on my phone and no idea what to search for.

What’s For Dinner Tonight

It started as a Notes file my wife and I were both editing every Sunday and never quite agreeing on.

GoSosh

I wanted to know what was happening in the world without scrolling for it.

TotalVu

I wondered how many of my photos existed only on an old drive in a drawer. The answer turned out to be sixteen thousand.

Sandeeze

My work scheduling system doesn’t talk to Apple Calendar, and I was tired of typing my schedule by hand.

Staggr

It came from the paper break board at the front of a busy store: a highlighter, a dozen names, and no way to see coverage at a glance.

Dunow

It began as a way to show off the fold: one screen, two people facing each other, racing to obey a command before the buzzer.
